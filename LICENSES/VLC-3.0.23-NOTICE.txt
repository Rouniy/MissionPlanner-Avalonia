VLC media player 3.0.23 macOS runtime
=====================================

The macOS Mission Planner artifacts redistribute the unmodified libvlc, libvlccore, plugin and
runtime-data directories extracted from the official VLC media player 3.0.23 application images.
The original lib/plugins/share sibling layout is preserved. Mission Planner does not redistribute
the VLC user interface or executable.

VLC and its modules are copyright their respective VideoLAN authors and contributors. libvlc and
libvlccore are licensed under GNU LGPL version 2.1 or later; VLC modules carry the GPL/LGPL and
compatible dependency licenses identified in the corresponding source. The authoritative license
texts are included in that source as COPYING and COPYING.LIB:
https://code.videolan.org/videolan/vlc/-/blob/3.0.23/COPYING
https://code.videolan.org/videolan/vlc/-/blob/3.0.23/COPYING.LIB

The build downloads over HTTPS, requires the exact architecture-specific archive size and SHA-256,
checks the extracted libvlc/libvlccore/plugin-cache hashes, rejects any dylib of the wrong Mach-O
architecture and records a complete runtime-file checksum manifest. See build/vlc/fetch-macos.sh.
